Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Overview

Brain Rot Meter is committed to protecting your privacy. We do not sell your data. We do not share your data. We collect only what is necessary to provide core app functionality.

Information We Collect

To function, the app may collect or process:

  • App usage statistics (how often the app is opened)
  • Reel and Short count statistics (how many you've watched)
  • Device information required for core functionality (OS version, device model)

Information We Do Not Collect

Brain Rot Meter does not collect, access, or transmit:

  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Messages or communications
  • Photos or videos
  • Contacts or address book data
  • Content of any reels or shorts viewed

Data Storage

All statistics are stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers unless you explicitly enable a future cloud synchronization feature (if introduced). You may request deletion of any stored data by clearing the app's data in your Android settings or by contacting us.

Accessibility Services

Brain Rot Meter uses Android Accessibility Services solely to detect scrolling activity within supported apps (Instagram and YouTube) in order to count reels and shorts. This service does not read, record, or transmit any content displayed on your screen.

Third-Party Services

Brain Rot Meter is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Meta, or Google. Any trademarks mentioned belong to their respective owners.

Children's Privacy

Brain Rot Meter is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
