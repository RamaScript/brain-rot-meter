Frequently Asked
Questions

Brain Rot Meter is an Android application that quantifies your short-form video consumption. It monitors Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts swipes in real time, reflects your current state across 10 progressive brain health stages, and renders a floating overlay so you cannot escape the truth.

The app leverages an Android Accessibility Service strictly to recognize forward scroll gestures inside verified reel containers. It employs a 5-minute sliding window filter to reject duplicates when you scroll backward. No screen frames, camera feeds, audio, or text content are inspected or recorded.

Yes. Brain Rot Meter seamlessly detects YouTube Shorts alongside Instagram Reels. Separate statistics for each platform are logged locally so you can see which app claims more of your attention.

Your mind passes through 10 stages each calendar day: Stage 1 (0–10 reels, Healthy), Stage 2 (11–25, Slightly Distracted), Stage 3 (26–50, Brain Fog), Stage 4 (51–75, Sleepy Mode), Stage 5 (76–100, Overstimulated), Stage 6 (101–150, Fried), Stage 7 (151–250, Damaged), Stage 8 (251–400, Rotting), Stage 9 (401–600, Critical), and Stage 10 (601+ reels, Dead Brain). The counter resets every night at midnight.

Your data stays completely local on your device by default. We never ask for account credentials, passwords, or contacts. If you use the optional Friends feature, only your anonymized 6-character code and daily aggregate count are synced to Firestore.

Brain Rot Meter is 100% free with no advertisements, no paywalls, and no subscription tiers. Digital awareness should be accessible to everyone.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. Commitment to Privacy

Brain Rot Meter is engineered with a strict privacy-first philosophy. We do not sell your personal data. We do not transmit your browsing history or camera/audio feeds.

2. Information We Process

To deliver real-time counting functionality, the app processes:

  • Reel and Short scroll events detected locally on your device
  • Aggregate daily count metrics and stage milestones (Stages 1 through 10)
  • Optional 6-character friend code and leaderboard pairing tokens if you use the social tab

3. Accessibility Service Usage

Android Accessibility Services are utilized solely to observe window state changes and vertical scroll events within supported video feeds (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts). This service never inspects video playback frames, user messages, typed keys, or personal interactions.

4. Data Storage & Deletion

All core statistics reside securely in local device storage. You can wipe all app data at any moment through Android Application Settings. For data inquiries, reach out to contact@ramascript.com.
