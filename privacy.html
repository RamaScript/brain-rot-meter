3. Android AccessibilityService API Disclosure

To detect short-form video consumption automatically without requiring root permissions, Brain Rot Meter requires the Android AccessibilityService permission.

  • Strict Purpose: The AccessibilityService is utilized exclusively to inspect window transition events and vertical scroll gestures inside supported video applications (specifically Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) to detect when a new short-form video has been swiped into view.
  • No Personal Data Captured: The service does NOT read, capture, monitor, log, or transmit personal messages, keystrokes, passwords, usernames, account credentials, payment card numbers, contact lists, photos, microphone audio, camera streams, or video content.
  • On-Device Only: Event recognition heuristics run strictly inside the local device sandbox. Raw accessibility event data is discarded immediately after incrementing the local numeric counter.
  • Voluntary & Revocable: Enabling AccessibilityService is entirely voluntary. You may disable or revoke this permission at any moment via your device's Android Settings → Accessibility → Brain Rot Meter.

4. Data Storage & Processing (Local-First Architecture)

All core counters, platform breakdowns (Instagram vs YouTube), daily historical logs, active session timers, and stage achievements are saved exclusively to your device's private application storage (Android Room Database / Encrypted DataStore). This data remains strictly on your device and is not uploaded to our servers during standard offline use.

5. Optional Cloud Leaderboard (Friends Sync)

If you choose to use the optional Friends Leaderboard feature, the App synchronizes minimal non-personally identifiable operational tokens with our cloud database (Google Cloud Firestore):

  • A randomly generated 6-character alphanumeric friend code.
  • A custom display nickname of your choice.
  • Your current daily reel count and calculated brain stage (Stages 1 through 10).
  • A timestamp of last activity.

No personal identifiers: We do NOT collect your email address, phone number, real name, device IMEI, Android ID, or GPS location for the Friends feature. You can disconnect or cease syncing at any time.
